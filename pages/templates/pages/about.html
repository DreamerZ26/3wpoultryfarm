About 3W Poultry Farm

We are a family‑owned farm dedicated to raising healthy poultry and producing the freshest eggs in the region.

Our journey began in 2010 with a vision to provide communities with high‑quality, ethically sourced poultry products. Today, we supply local markets, restaurants, and families with eggs and meat that are free from hormones and antibiotics.

We believe in sustainable farming practices that respect the environment and animal welfare.
